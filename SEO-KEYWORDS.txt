GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE — STEP-BY-STEP SUBMISSION
================================================================

STEP 1 — Deploy
   Push to your Vercel project. Confirm
   https://rasheedandassociateslahore.me loads with the new pages.
   Test 3–4 deep links directly in the browser, e.g.
   https://rasheedandassociateslahore.me/practice/criminal

STEP 2 — Add property in Search Console
   Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
   Click "Add property" → choose "URL prefix"
   Enter exactly: https://rasheedandassociateslahore.me

STEP 3 — Verify ownership
   Easiest method: HTML tag verification.
   GSC will give you a meta tag like:
      <meta name="google-site-verification" content="AbCdEf123..." />
   Take the content value (just the string), open
   app/layout.tsx, find the metadata object, and add:

      verification: { google: 'AbCdEf123...' },

   (The placeholder is already there from info.txt — replace it.)
   Push to Vercel, wait 1–2 min, then click "Verify" in GSC.

STEP 4 — Submit your sitemap
   In GSC left sidebar: Sitemaps → "Add a new sitemap"
   Enter:  sitemap.xml
   (Full URL becomes https://rasheedandassociateslahore.me/sitemap.xml)
   Click Submit. Within 24 hours status should show "Success".

STEP 5 — Request indexing for top 10 pages manually
   Use the URL Inspection tool (top of GSC). Paste each URL,
   then click "Request indexing". Do these first, in order:
      1. https://rasheedandassociateslahore.me/
      2. https://rasheedandassociateslahore.me/lahore-lawyer
      3. https://rasheedandassociateslahore.me/lahore-high-court-advocate
      4. https://rasheedandassociateslahore.me/practice/criminal
      5. https://rasheedandassociateslahore.me/practice/property
      6. https://rasheedandassociateslahore.me/practice/family

   the sitemap will pick up the rest within a week.
